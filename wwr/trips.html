Our Trips

Family fun Rafting

Perfect for first-timers, kids, and big groups!Take it easy and enjoy the beauty of the canyon. Our Family Fun trip is a relaxing float down gentle Class I-II rapids. It's all about laughter, swimming, and making memories together. Our guides do all the work so you can just enjoy the ride.Includes: Safety gear, professional guide, light snacks & water Duration: 3 Hours | Ages: 6+ | Difficulty: Easy

White Water Adventure

Our most popular trip - The perfect mix of thrill and scenery!Looking for more excitement? This full-day adventure takes you through awesome Class III-IV rapids with breathtaking canyon views in between. You'll get wet, you'll paddle hard, and you'll have the time of your life. Includes a delicious riverside lunch.Includes: All gear, expert guide, lunch & photos Duration: 7 Hours | Ages: 12+ | Difficulty: Intermediate

Extrene Rapids Challenge

For adrenaline junkies only. Are you brave enough?This is not for the faint of heart. Conquer the legendary Devil's Gorge with massive, non-stop Class IV-V rapids. This intense 2-day expedition with overnight camping will push you to your limits. Previous experience required.Includes: All rafting & camping gear, all meals, pro guides Duration: 2 Days | Ages: 18+ | Difficulty: Expert / Extreme
